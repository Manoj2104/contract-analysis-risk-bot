

This Agreement is executed on 01 May 2025 between the following parties:

 Employer

ABC Technologies Private Limited,
having its registered office at Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

 Employee

Mr. Rahul Kumar,
Resident of Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.


 1. Appointment

The Employer appoints the Employee to the position of Software Engineer, and the Employee accepts this appointment.


This Agreement shall be valid for a period of 12 months, commencing from 01 May 2025 and ending on 30 April 2026.



The Employee shall be paid a salary of ₹50,000 per month, payable at the convenience of the Employer.
The Employer may, without assigning any reason, defer or partially withhold the payment of salary.




 4. Duties and Responsibilities

The Employee shall perform assigned duties and any additional tasks, whether or not related to the Employee’s role, without additional remuneration.




 5. Confidentiality

The Employee shall not disclose confidential information.
In case of breach, the Employee shall be fully liable for all direct and indirect losses suffered by the Company.




 6. Termination of Agreement

The Employer may terminate this Agreement at any time, with immediate effect, without notice or reason.
The Employee shall not be entitled to compensation or outstanding payments.



 7. Non-Compete Clause

The Employee shall not engage in similar or competing business during employment and for 3 years after termination, in India or abroad.


 8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by Indian law, and only courts in Chennai shall have jurisdiction, regardless of employee residence.


 9. Absence of Limitation of Liability

The Employer shall have no limitation of liability, while the Employee’s liability shall be unlimited.
