1. Appointment & Designation
1.	The Company hereby appoints the Employee as Data Analyst.
2.	The Employee agrees to perform all assigned duties sincerely, efficiently, and in the best interest of the Company.
3.	The Employee shall report to Mr. Arjun Mehta, Project Manager.

2. Nature of Employment
1.	The employment shall be full-time and permanent.
2.	This Agreement does not create any partnership, agency, or contractor relationship.
3.	The Employee shall devote full working time and attention to Company duties.

3. Date of Commencement & Term
1.	Employment shall commence on 01 March 2026.
2.	This Agreement shall remain in force unless terminated as per Clause 13.

4. Work Location & Working Hours
1.	The primary work location shall be Chennai Office, with occasional remote work as approved.
2.	Working hours shall be 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
3.	Additional working hours may be required depending on business needs, without overtime compensation unless mandated by law.

5. Duties & Responsibilities
The Employee shall:
•	Analyze and interpret business data
•	Prepare reports and dashboards
•	Maintain data confidentiality
•	Follow Company policies and instructions
•	Meet project deadlines and performance expectations
•	Avoid conflicts of interest

6. Compensation & Benefits
1.	The Employee shall receive a salary of ₹6,00,000 (Rupees Six Lakhs Only) per annum, payable monthly.
2.	Salary is subject to income tax and statutory deductions.
3.	Additional benefits include:
o	Performance bonus (discretionary)
o	Health insurance
o	Paid leaves as per Company policy

7. Leave Policy
1.	The Employee is entitled to:
o	12 Paid Leaves per year
o	8 Casual Leaves per year
o	6 Sick Leaves per year
2.	Leave approval must be obtained in advance except in emergencies.
3.	Unused leave shall lapse at the end of the calendar year unless otherwise stated.

8. Confidentiality
1.	The Employee shall maintain strict confidentiality of all Company information including:
o	Client details
o	Financial data
o	Internal documents
o	Software, models, and source code
2.	This obligation shall continue even after termination.
3.	Any breach shall attract disciplinary and legal action.

9. Intellectual Property Rights
1.	All intellectual property created during employment shall be the exclusive property of the Company.
2.	This includes reports, software, algorithms, designs, documents, and research work.
3.	The Employee waives any ownership or moral rights over such work.

10. Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation
1.	During employment, the Employee shall not engage in any competing business.
2.	For 6 months after termination, the Employee shall not:
o	Solicit Company clients
o	Poach Company employees
o	Use Company trade secrets
3.	These restrictions shall apply as permitted under applicable law.

11. Code of Conduct
The Employee agrees to:
•	Act professionally and ethically
•	Avoid harassment, discrimination, or misconduct
•	Protect Company reputation
•	Use Company resources responsibly

12. Performance Evaluation
1.	Performance shall be reviewed quarterly.
2.	Continued employment depends on satisfactory performance.
3.	The Company may initiate corrective or disciplinary action if required.

13. Termination
1.	Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days’ written notice.
2.	The Company may terminate employment immediately without notice in case of:
o	Gross misconduct
o	Fraud or theft
o	Breach of confidentiality
o	Willful negligence
3.	Upon termination, all Company assets must be returned.

14. Notice Period & Final Settlement
1.	Notice period may be waived by the Company at its discretion.
2.	Final settlement shall include:
o	Salary till last working day
o	Approved reimbursements
o	Statutory deductions

15. Governing Law & Jurisdiction
1.	This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
2.	Courts of Chennai, Tamil Nadu shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

16. Amendments
Any changes to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

17. Severability
If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue to be enforceable.

18. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the complete understanding between the Parties and supersedes all prior communications.
